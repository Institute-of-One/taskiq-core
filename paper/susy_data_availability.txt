The data presented in this study are openly available. The code that generates every number, table and figure - the phantom generators, the physical and model-observer estimators, the injection study and the test suite - is at https://github.com/Institute-of-One/taskiq-core under the MIT licence and archived on Zenodo, version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21422924 (v0.3.0); concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21422923 resolves to the latest version. The generated results are written to paper/figures/injection.json and paper/results/acr_atlas.json by the scripts that produce the figures, so the text and the figures cannot diverge. The measured projections analysed in Sections 3.7 and 3.8 are the ACR_Phantom series of LDCT-and-Projection-data, The Cancer Imaging Archive, CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.7937/9npb-2637; they were not generated by this study and are redistributed by the archive under that licence.
